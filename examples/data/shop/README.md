# Data card: the shop's data

The book's running case is Fernhill Supply, a fictional online shop for kitchen and garden goods. It stands for no real business. All of its records are generated by `examples/lib/shop_data.py` from one fixed seed, `SHOP_SEED = 4217`, with Python's `random.Random`, and held in memory: nothing here is drawn from a real business, a person or anyone's employer, and no generated records are committed.

- **Generator:** `lib.shop_data.load_shop()`. Each kind of record has its own random stream, so the records of one kind never change when another kind's generation changes.
- **Reference date:** the data ends on 2026-06-30 and never depends on the date it runs.
- **Licence:** the generator is example code, MIT like every example. The generated data, and the documents and templates in this folder, are CC BY 4.0 with the book's text.

## Fields

| Record | Count | Fields |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Customer` | 240 | `customer_id` (`C-0042`), `display_name` (a given name from a fixed list and an initial), `email` (`customer-0042@example.com`), `country`, `phone` (masked: `•••• ••42`) |
| `Product` | 64 | `sku` (`KG-1031`), `name`, `category` (6), `price_cents`, `weight_g`, `popularity` (skewed) |
| `StockLevel` | 128 | `sku`, `warehouse` (`north`, `south`), `on_hand` (a few negative), `last_counted` (a few stale) |
| `Order` | 1,500 | `order_id` (`O-1001` to `O-2500`), `customer_id`, `placed_on`, `placed_at`, `status`, `lines`, `shipping_cents`, `total_cents`, `shipping_method`, `shipped_on`, `delivered_on`, `payment` (`card ending 4242`) |
| `Refund` | 110 | `refund_id`, `order_id`, `amount_cents`, `reason`, `status`, `requested_on`, `policy_limit_cents`, `exceeds_limit` |
| `Ticket` | 48 | `ticket_id`, `customer_id`, `received_at`, `subject`, `body`, `expected_tool`, `order_id`, `cited_order_id`, `sku`, `injected` |

Order statuses are `placed`, `shipped`, `delivered`, `cancelled` and `returned`. Money is in euros. The generator keeps integer cents, and the shop's API sends amounts as plain decimals with no currency code (`"180.00"`). Refunds above the policy limit (`REFUND_LIMIT_CENTS`, €150.00) need a person's confirmation. Tickets are written from templates with typos, pasted order lines, uneven politeness and some wrong order IDs; each is tagged with the tool a correct agent calls first, and six carry instructions aimed at an agent.

## Policies

Six short documents written for the book, in `policies/`, which chapter 4 serves as resources at `shop://policies/{slug}`. They are licensed CC BY 4.0 with the book's text, and they describe the fictional shop: no real business's terms. The policy documents still write amounts with a `$` sign; they move to euros with the next re-recording of the book's model calls, because their text is part of the recorded prompts. Each file opens with its title, an `Updated:` date (fixed, never today's) and a one-sentence summary, which `lib.policies.load_policies()` reads.

| File | Policy | What it covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `refund.md` | Refund policy | who qualifies (30 days from delivery), how much, the €150.00 limit that needs a support lead |
| `returns.md` | Returns policy | the 30-day window, the items' condition, who pays to send them back |
| `shipping.md` | Shipping policy | the six countries, standard and express prices and times, when a parcel is late or lost |
| `stock.md` | Stock policy | reading per-warehouse counts, negative and stale counts, what to tell a customer |
| `data-handling.md` | Data-handling policy | whose data may be discussed, what may be repeated, what is kept |
| `escalation.md` | Escalation policy | when a ticket goes to a support lead, and how to hand it over |

The policies' numbers match the generator: `REFUND_LIMIT_CENTS`, the shipping prices and free-shipping threshold in `_orders()`, refunds requested within 20 days of delivery, and the two warehouses.

## Privacy

Emails are only at `example.com`; hosts are `fernhill.example`, `partner.example` and `127.0.0.1`; phones are masked to two digits; payment is "card ending" and the last four digits of a published test card number; there are no IBANs or full card numbers.
