# Stock policy

Updated: 2026-06-01

How to read the shop's stock counts, and what to tell a customer who asks whether something is in stock.

## Reading a count

- Each product has an on-hand count in each warehouse, `north` and `south`, and the date it was last counted.
- A product is in stock when the two warehouses together hold at least one.
- A negative count means the shop sold items that a recount later found missing. Treat it as zero, and ask the warehouse for a recount.
- A count last made more than 90 days ago is stale. Say the product is probably in stock, never certainly, and ask for a recount.

## What to tell a customer

- Say whether the product is in stock, not the exact number on hand.
- Never promise a restock date. Offer to let the customer know when it is back.
- When only one warehouse holds the product, an order may take one more working day to dispatch.
