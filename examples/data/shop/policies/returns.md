# Returns policy

Updated: 2026-05-04

How a customer sends goods back, and what condition they must be in.

## The window

Customers may return any item within 30 days of the delivery date. After 30 days, only faulty items are taken back, and a support lead decides.

## Condition

- Unused items come back in their original packaging.
- Cookware and bakeware that have been heated, and kitchen tools that have touched food, are returned only if they are faulty.
- Planters and raised beds over 20 kg are collected by the courier rather than posted: the support team books the collection.

## Who pays to send it back

The customer pays return postage, unless the return is the shop's mistake: damaged in transit, wrong item sent or a missing part. Then the shop sends a prepaid label.

## What happens next

The warehouse checks the item within 3 working days of receiving it. The order's status becomes `returned`, and the refund policy decides the amount. Partial returns keep the order `delivered` and refund only the lines that came back.
