# Shipping policy

Updated: 2026-03-16

What delivery costs, how long it takes, and when a parcel counts as late.

## Where the shop ships

The shop ships to Great Britain, Ireland, the Netherlands, Germany, France and Belgium, from two warehouses, `north` and `south`. One order may leave from both.

## Methods and prices

| Method | Price | Delivery after dispatch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| standard | $4.99, free for goods of $50.00 or more | 2 to 6 working days |
| express | $9.00 more than standard | 1 to 3 working days |

Orders are dispatched within 3 working days of being placed. The order's status becomes `shipped` on the dispatch date and `delivered` when the courier confirms delivery.

## Late parcels

- A standard parcel is late 8 working days after dispatch, and an express parcel 4.
- A late parcel is traced with the courier first. If the courier can't find it within 2 more working days, it is lost: the support team sends a replacement or refunds the order, and the customer chooses.
- Express shipping is refunded on any late express parcel.
