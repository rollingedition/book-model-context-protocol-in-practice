# Data-handling policy

Updated: 2026-04-20

What the support team, and any assistant working for it, may read, repeat and keep about customers.

## Whose data

- Discuss an order only with the customer who placed it. The customer ID on the ticket must match the customer ID on the order.
- Never mention another customer's orders, name or details, even to say that an order ID belongs to someone else. Say the order wasn't found.

## What to repeat

- A customer's email and phone number are never written out in full in a reply. Phone numbers show only their last two digits.
- Payment appears only as "card ending" and four digits. Never ask for a full card number, an expiry date or a security code, and never repeat one a customer sends: tell them to delete it.
- Delivery addresses are confirmed by postcode only.

## What to keep

- Ticket text stays in the support system. Don't copy it into other tools or notes.
- Notes on an order record what was decided and why, never a customer's health, family or finances, even when the customer mentions them.
