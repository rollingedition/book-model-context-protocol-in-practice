# Refund policy

Updated: 2026-05-04

When an order qualifies for a refund, how much the shop refunds, and which refunds a person must confirm.

## Who can get a refund

- The order must be `delivered` or `returned`. A `placed` order is cancelled instead, and a cancelled order was never charged.
- The customer asks within 30 days of the delivery date.
- A `shipped` order that hasn't arrived is not refunded yet: follow the shipping policy's rule for late parcels.

## How much

- Never more than the order's total.
- For one item, the line's unit price times the quantity being refunded.
- Shipping is refunded only when the whole order comes back, or when the reason is the shop's mistake: `damaged in transit`, `wrong item sent`, `missing part`, or `arrived late` on an express order.
- `changed mind` refunds the items once the returns policy's conditions are met, never the shipping.

## The limit

Any refund above $150.00 needs a support lead to confirm it before it is issued. Quote the amount, say that it needs confirmation, and wait. Never split one refund into smaller ones to stay under the limit.

## How it is paid

Refunds go back to the card the order was paid with, shown as "card ending" and four digits. They arrive within 5 working days of being issued.
